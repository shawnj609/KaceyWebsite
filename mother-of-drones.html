Drone Artist

I'm a drone light show designer and creative technologist pushing the boundaries of what's possible with aerial choreography. With over a decade in entertainment lighting design, including tours and architectural installations, I transitioned into drone shows in twenty twenty-two. At Verge Aero, I design large-scale, high-profile productions for prestigious brands, concert tours, and other organizations. Beyond that work, I'm driven by experimental storytelling--creating intimate interactive installations with minimal drone counts, integrating aerial choreography with live dance and other artistic mediums, and exploring unconventional ways to use light and motion to move audiences.
