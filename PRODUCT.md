# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors arrive referred — casting directors, producers, festival programmers, event bookers, and press who already have Kacey's name from someone they trust. They are here to verify, not to discover: their job is confirming the referral was right and finding the next step.

## Product Purpose

A calling-card portfolio for Kacey Samiee — actor, filmmaker, drone-light-show designer, fire dancer. Because visitors already have her name, the site's job is legitimacy rather than cold conversion: confirm that the person they heard about is real, serious, and better than they were told. Success is a visitor who leans in, believes the work, and sends a booking or press inquiry.

## Positioning

The portfolio that withholds. Every page shows less than it could — film title-sequence logic — so the visitor leaves with the one feeling the site exists to create: *who is this person? I want more.*

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: **"Bookings & Press Inquiries"** — an email contact. The public address is not yet chosen; the site currently ships no contact path at all, so placing this CTA is the first open build task.
- Secondary CTA: **"Observe"** — a follow link for visitors not ready to reach out. The platform and handle are Kacey's to supply; no social accounts are linked anywhere today.
- The line a visitor remembers: not a printed tagline but a felt question — unattainable mystery. *"Who is she? I want more."*
- Belief ladder: **Intrigue** (this is unlike other portfolios; lean in) → **Proof** (the work backs up the mystery: reels, shows, laurels) → **Desire** (I want to be part of whatever this is — inquire).
- Proof on hand: festival selections, press coverage, collaborator quotes, and named shows/venues all exist, but none are gathered in the repo yet. Collect them into `.impeccable/assets/proof/` before building any proof surface.

## Brand Personality

"Mysterious with a touch of danger" — the brief, verbatim. Composed, cinematic, withholding; the danger is implied, never costumed. The locked direction is **"The Cold Open"**: film title-sequence logic where the page shows less than it could and makes you lean in. The monospace voice (nav, buttons, captions, metadata) is justified by screenplay and call-sheet convention — it never carries body copy and never decorates section headings.

## Anti-references

- The Squarespace actor template: headshot hero, résumé grid, credits table, "represented by" footer. Competent and forgettable.
- Influencer energy: Linktree patterns, follower counts, collab-bait copy, platform logos. The exact opposite of unattainable.
- The event-vendor site: packages, quote forms, "trusted by" logo rows, FAQ accordions — the drone-show-company look that turns an artist into a supplier.
- Edgelord goth: danger overcooked into skulls, blood reds, and distressed grunge type. The menace here stays elegant.

## Design Principles

1. **Build on the shipped film, never over it.** The cinematic intros on the home and Mother of Drones pages are the art direction. Design work adds type, structure, and components on top of the videos — it never mocks over them with stills.
2. **Withhold to intrigue.** Every surface earns lean-in by showing less than it could. When in doubt, cut; an unexplained image outperforms an explained one.
3. **Menace stays elegant.** Danger reads through composure — black, hairlines, fire and light in the footage — never through horror costume.
4. **Proof reads as credits.** Laurels, press, and quotes appear with title-sequence and call-sheet logic, set like film credits — never as testimonial carousels or logo rows.
5. **Confirm, don't sell.** Visitors arrive referred; the tone assumes they already believe a little and never chases them like a cold lead.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal conformance target, by owner decision (2026-08-06). The accessibility work already shipped in site.css — reduced-motion alternatives for every animation and visible focus states on all interactive elements — remains in place as part of the existing code.
