“Stuff I Made” — three treatments

Today the divider uses the exact same stacked-serif-with-one-gold-word grammar as “Photo Gallery” and “Reel Gallery,” so it reads as another sibling section instead of a chapter break announcing “everything below this, she wrote, directed & produced.” Each option breaks that ambiguity a different way. All three are built on the live page and stylesheet — scroll from the reel gallery into Anita to feel the transition.

A — The Second Cold Open

View →

The divider stops speaking section-heading and starts speaking title-card: one spread line of tracked caps, the hairline rule, and a mono credit line — “WRITTEN · DIRECTED · PRODUCED.” The same grammar as the page's own hero, so it reads as a new film beginning. Biggest ceremony, zero new components.

B — The Billing Block

View →

A main-title billing card: the heading, a mono role credit naming her outright (“written · directed · produced by Kacey Samiee”), then the three films listed like a title-sequence contents slate. Each film name jump-links to its section — the most explicit “here is what follows.”

C — The Slate

View →

The divider shrinks to a quiet full-bleed band between hairlines, and the credit moves down into every project heading: title, mini rule, mono roles — each film gets its own title-card. Roles can differ per project (Blood Sisters shows a placeholder variation), so the credits stay honest as the filmography grows.

D — The Credit Alone

View →

Option A stripped to its last line: no “Stuff I Made,” no rule — just the mono credit “WRITTEN · DIRECTED · PRODUCED” at three times its normal size, alone in the dark. The call sheet becomes the title card; the roles are the whole announcement.

Role lines are placeholders — swap in real credits per film.
